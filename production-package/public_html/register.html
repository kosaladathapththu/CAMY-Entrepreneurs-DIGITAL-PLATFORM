Become a CAMY entrepreneur in Sri Lanka

Apply online to build your product-selling business with CAMY. Approved entrepreneurs receive a secure digital workspace for customer orders, delivery tracking, commissions and business growth.

Start your CAMY entrepreneur application

  • Complete your personal and contact details.
  • Tell CAMY about your selling experience and business goals.
  • Upload clear front and back images of your NIC.
  • CAMY reviews your application before activating your account.
